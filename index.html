USE YOUR RESCUE INHALER

if you have a rescue/reliever inhaler prescribed, use it now according to your action plan (usually 1-2 puffs).

GUIDED BREATHING

try to sync your breath with the circle pulse. please breathe in when they expand and breathe in when they become smaller.



breathe in slowly...

race driver position (Kutschersitz)

sit down and lean forward, then rest your elbows on your knees and let your shoulders drop.

pursed lip-breathing (Lippenbremse)

breathe in through your nose and then try to exhale slowly and gently through loosely puckered lips ("pursed lips"). you dont need to press your lips together.

⚠️ Important Safety Note:

if your attack persists for more than 10 minutes or if your rescue inhaler does not help, call emergency services immediately (such as 112 or 911 depending on your location).
